## Overview

A marketing website for Keshav Packers and Movers, a local relocation and
logistics business, built as a part-time client project. The goal was
straightforward: give the business a credible online presence and a way
for potential customers to reach out for quotes, replacing word-of-mouth
as the only channel.

## Approach

The site is built with Next.js and Tailwind CSS, with Framer Motion used
for page and scroll animations to make the site feel more polished than
a typical small-business template. A contact flow sends inquiry details
by email via Nodemailer, so leads land directly in the owner's inbox
without needing a separate backend or dashboard.

I handled requirements, design, and implementation directly with the
client, keeping the stack intentionally simple so the site would be easy
to deploy and maintain going forward.

## Stack

- **Next.js** for the frontend
- **Tailwind CSS** for styling
- **Framer Motion** for animation
- **Nodemailer** for the contact/quote request flow
- **Vercel** for deployment
