*This article is my opinion, with facts, on the war between Russia and Ukraine. You may agree or disagree with it, and I respect both.*

To understand this, we first need to know about NATO and the USSR. NATO is the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, and the USSR is the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics.

After World War II, the countries of Europe feared that the USSR would again follow an expansionist policy and take territory in Europe. That could lead to another bloody war and badly damage their economies. To ease this fear, twelve countries formed NATO in April 1949 with the support of the United States: the United States, Canada, Belgium, Denmark, France, Iceland, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal and the United Kingdom.

The USSR was not happy with this grouping. It argued through diplomatic channels that NATO was just a front for American imperialism to counter the Soviets. In October 1949, about six months after NATO was formed, the USSR created East Germany out of the part of Germany it had occupied after the war. In 1954, the USSR even proposed joining NATO to maintain peace in Europe, but the Western powers rejected the offer. They believed it was a way to find weaknesses in NATO from within. When West Germany joined NATO in May 1955, the USSR responded by forming the Warsaw Pact the same month. Its members were Albania, Bulgaria, Czechoslovakia, East Germany, Hungary, Poland, Romania and the Soviet Union. From this we can see that the USSR was never comfortable with NATO or with its growth close to Soviet borders.

The Cold War ended when the USSR collapsed in December 1991, and Ukraine became an independent country. After that, many former Warsaw Pact countries joined NATO: Poland, Hungary and the Czech Republic in 1999, followed in 2004 by more countries, including the Baltic states, which were once part of the USSR. At its 2008 summit in Bucharest, NATO said that Ukraine and Georgia would become members one day, though it gave no timeline. In 2014, protests in Kyiv removed Ukraine's pro-Russian president. Russia then took over Crimea and backed separatists in eastern Ukraine. On 24 February 2022, Russia launched a full-scale invasion.

Now you might think NATO is just Europe protecting itself from Russia. But if you look from Russia's side, the story is different. The USA, the Cold War rival of the USSR, has also been a brutal power. It has carried out coups and wars in other countries, and many people have died, often over oil. Iran, Iraq and Syria are examples. Russia has done similar things too, but because it is not as strong, the damage has been more limited. If it had more power, it might have done the same.

So when Ukraine, a neighbour of Russia, moved toward joining NATO, it was the last straw. Russia does not want American troops and missiles next to its border, with its big cities within range of US weapons. That is why it attacked Ukraine, and it is now stuck in a long war.
